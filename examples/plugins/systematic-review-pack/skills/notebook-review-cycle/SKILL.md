# Notebook review cycle

Read `criteria.md` before searching. Record candidates that fail the criteria
with `flip pass`; capture and judge candidates that survive. Promote a claim
only when the brief relies on it. In synthesis, distinguish source statements,
your inference, and absence. Finish with `flip doctor` and close the session
with the actual durable outputs and checks, without treating a clean fixture as
proof the review is useful or complete.
