**Question.** How should the site argue for flip when there is no adoption evidence?

**Decision.** Argue from mechanical proof: real CLI output, real conformance, real refusals — and state the absence of adoption evidence plainly.

**Why.** The lowest layer of the decision stack is observed user behaviour, and it is empty. Design cannot manufacture it, and implying a user base we do not have would forfeit the one thing the project is actually about.

**Rejected.** Case studies written from the author's own private notebooks — unverifiable by a reader and self-interested; Waiting to launch the site until adoption exists — the site is how adoption starts
